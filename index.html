     MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

    Explore the studio.

    Orbit mode
    Drag to turn around the scene. Scroll or pinch to move closer.
    Walk mode
    Move the mouse to look; no dragging needed. W A S D or arrow keys move you in that direction. Click a destination or press E when aiming at it. Esc frees the cursor. On touchscreens, drag to look and use the direction pad.
    Explore connected MEP
    At the model table, choose Connections to trace the AHU systems, or Challenge to compare cable tray routes. Refocus returns to the best inspection view.
    Select an object
    Click the building model, workstation, or project wall. Their labels and the area menu work too.
    Find your way back
    Choose Resume exploring or press Escape to return to your previous position. The GE logo and Studio overview reset the view.

    Automatic adjusts resolution for smoother movement, up to 1080p. Choose a fixed resolution if you prefer. Resolution follows the window shape.
